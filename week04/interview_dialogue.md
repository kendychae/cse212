# Week 4 Interview Dialogue Script - SIMPLIFIED

**Duration: ~1 minute**

---

## Introduction (5 seconds)

"Hi! I'll explain how to build a Stack and Queue using a linked list."

---

## Stack Implementation (25 seconds)

"For a **Stack**, think of it like a pile of plates. You add new plates on top and take plates from the top.

With a linked list, I use the **head as the top**:

**Push**: Add a new item at the head. Fast - takes the same time no matter how big the stack is.

**Pop**: Remove the item at the head. Also fast - just move the head pointer.

**GetTop**: Look at the head item without removing it. Fast.

**IsEmpty**: Check if the head is empty. Fast.

Everything happens at the head, so it's all very quick."

---

## Queue Implementation (25 seconds)

"For a **Queue**, think of a line at a store. First person in line is first to be served.

With a linked list, I use **both ends**:

**Enqueue**: Add new people at the tail (back of line). Fast.

**Dequeue**: Remove people from the head (front of line). Fast.

**Size**: Keep a simple counter. Fast.

**IsEmpty**: Check if the head is empty. Fast.

Using both ends makes everything quick because we don't have to walk through the whole list."

---

## Conclusion (5 seconds)

"So linked lists work great for both stacks and queues because we can access both ends quickly. Thanks!"

---

## Speaking Tips:

- Use **simple analogies** (plates, store line)
- Speak **slowly and clearly**
- **Smile** and stay relaxed
- **Pause briefly** between sections
